Ball Drop Game

Sort colored balls by dragging them between columns. Plan your moves carefully - you can only move the top ball from any column, and you must place it on top of another column. The goal is to arrange all balls of the same color into single columns. The fewer moves you make, the better your score!

Towers of Hanoi

Move all disks from the left rod to the right rod. Only one disk can be moved at a time, and you can never place a larger disk on top of a smaller one. Challenge yourself with different difficulty levels and plan each move to solve the puzzle in the fewest moves possible!
